# Security and trust boundary

IntentSeal is a research prototype.

Do not deploy the archived Verification Kernel or VRK as a production authorization, payment, safety, or scientific-truth boundary without an independent security review.

The repository deliberately separates two questions:

- Is an action authorized?
- Is a scientific claim supported by its required evidence?

An authority receipt cannot promote scientific evidence, and a scientific certificate cannot authorize a privileged action.

When reporting a problem, identify the exact version, artifact SHA-256, claim or receipt digest, and the minimal reproducer. Preserve failed or adversarial cases; do not silently repair frozen artifacts.
